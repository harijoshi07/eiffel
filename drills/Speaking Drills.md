# Speaking Drills

Record every attempt. Replay before moving on. Silent practice does not count.

---

## Task 1 — Listen and Repeat

**How to use these drills:**
1. Read the sentence silently **once**.
2. Cover it.
3. Say it aloud and record.
4. Compare your recording against the original.
5. Mark each error type: `omission` · `grammar` · `wrong word` · `unclear sound` · `broken rhythm`.
6. Repeat once correctly, then move on. Do not repeat more than twice.

**Recovery rule:** If one word disappears, keep speaking. Preserve meaning and key content words. A brief gap is far less damaging than a full restart.

---

### Set A — Short sentences (15 items)

1. The registration deadline is Friday at 5 p.m.
2. Students who miss class must contact the instructor directly.
3. Please submit your application through the online portal.
4. The library will be closed for maintenance next Monday.
5. The results of the study were published last month.
6. You should review your notes before the exam tomorrow.
7. The meeting has been moved to the conference room on the third floor.
8. Applicants must provide two letters of recommendation.
9. The seminar begins at 10 a.m. and runs for approximately two hours.
10. Please turn off your mobile devices before the session starts.
11. The scholarship is available to full-time students only.
12. She asked whether the assignment could be submitted late.
13. The committee will announce its decision by the end of the week.
14. Participants are expected to read the materials in advance.
15. The course covers both theoretical and practical aspects of the subject.

**What to listen for in yourself:**
- Do you finish plural and past-tense endings?
- Are content words stressed and function words reduced?
- Do you pause at phrase boundaries, not inside noun phrases?

### Set B — Medium sentences (15 items)

1. Students who have not yet submitted their fee payment should visit the bursar's office before Thursday.
2. The professor explained that the exam would cover all material discussed during the final three weeks of the semester.
3. Although the project deadline has been extended by one week, the quality of submissions is expected to remain the same.
4. Researchers found that participants who slept for at least seven hours recalled significantly more information the following morning.
5. The university will offer a series of free workshops on academic writing throughout the spring semester.
6. Could you please confirm whether the laboratory session on Wednesday has been moved or cancelled?
7. The student union is collecting donations for the local food bank until the end of this month.
8. Despite the heavy rain, more than two hundred students attended the outdoor graduation ceremony.
9. The government has introduced new regulations requiring companies to disclose their environmental impact annually.
10. The report concluded that greater investment in renewable energy would reduce carbon emissions over the next decade.
11. Participants should ensure that all required documents are submitted before the application portal closes.
12. The professor mentioned that the research paper should include at least five peer-reviewed academic sources.
13. Several students have already expressed interest in forming a study group for the upcoming mid-term examinations.
14. The career centre offers individual appointments for students who would like help preparing their resumes or cover letters.
15. The committee agreed that the most urgent priority was improving access to affordable housing in the city centre.

### Set C — Long complex sentences (10 items)

1. Although early research suggested that multitasking could improve productivity, more recent studies have consistently shown that switching between tasks reduces both accuracy and efficiency.
2. The new student support programme, which was launched at the beginning of this academic year, offers tutoring, counselling, and career guidance to all enrolled students free of charge.
3. Scientists studying the migratory patterns of monarch butterflies have discovered that the insects navigate using a combination of the sun's position and an internal magnetic compass.
4. The professor reminded her students that while secondary sources can provide useful context, all major claims in a research paper must be supported by primary evidence.
5. Despite significant advances in artificial intelligence, experts argue that human judgment remains essential for decisions involving ethical considerations, particularly in healthcare and law.
6. The funding proposal, which was submitted to the national research council last spring, outlined a three-year plan to develop affordable water purification technology for rural communities.
7. Linguists have long debated whether language shapes the way people perceive reality or whether thought processes are fundamentally the same across all cultures and languages.
8. The university's sustainability report revealed that campus energy consumption had decreased by 18 percent over the past five years, largely as a result of new solar panel installations.
9. Students who are applying for postgraduate programmes should be aware that most institutions require a statement of purpose, academic transcripts, and at least two letters of recommendation.
10. The city council voted to approve the construction of a new public library on the condition that the developers preserved the historic facade of the existing building on the site.

## Task 2 — Take an Interview

**Format:** 60 seconds per answer. Start within 2 seconds of reading the question. Use PREP structure.

```
P — Point:   answer the question directly in the first sentence
R — Reason:  explain the main reason or logic
E — Example: give a specific, concrete case
P — Point:   close or state a consequence
```

**If your answer feels too short**, add one rung of the development ladder:
answer → reason → example → consequence → brief contrast

---

### Self-review rubric (score each answer 0–2)

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intelligibility | often unclear | mostly understandable | consistently clear |
| Fluency | frequent breakdowns | some hesitation | steady phrasing |
| Language control | errors obscure meaning | noticeable but meaning clear | accurate and flexible |
| Relevance | misses question | generally relevant | direct and focused |
| Development | minimal / off-topic | some support | reason + specific example |

**Target per answer: 8 / 10.** Rerecord anything below 6.

---

### Set A — Preference questions (10 prompts)

Answer each in 60 seconds. Record and score.

1. Do you prefer studying alone or with a group? Why?
2. Would you rather live in a big city or a small town? Give reasons.
3. Do you prefer learning from books or from experience? Explain.
4. Would you rather have a job with a high salary or one you find meaningful? Why?
5. Do you prefer making plans in advance or acting spontaneously? Explain.
6. Would you rather take online classes or attend classes in person? Why?
7. Do you prefer spending free time with others or by yourself?
8. Would you rather travel abroad or explore your own country? Give reasons.
9. Do you prefer working on one task at a time or juggling several things at once?
10. Would you rather learn a new language or a new technical skill? Why?

---

### Set B — Past experience questions (10 prompts)

1. Describe a time you had to work under pressure. What happened and what did you learn?
2. Tell me about a person who has had a positive influence on your life.
3. Describe a challenge you overcame. How did you handle it?
4. Tell me about a time you had to adapt to an unfamiliar situation.
5. Describe a project you worked on that you are proud of.
6. Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned from it.
7. Describe a skill you taught yourself. How did you do it?
8. Tell me about a goal you set and achieved. What steps did you take?
9. Describe a time you disagreed with someone and how you resolved it.
10. Tell me about an experience that changed the way you think about something.

---

### Set C — Opinion and advice questions (10 prompts)

1. Do you think children should learn a second language at an early age? Why or why not?
2. Some people say that success depends more on hard work than on talent. Do you agree?
3. What advice would you give to someone starting university for the first time?
4. Is it better to specialise in one subject or to study a wide range of subjects? Why?
5. Some people believe that social media has more negative effects than positive ones. What do you think?
6. What is the most important quality for a good leader to have? Give reasons.
7. Do you think it is important to maintain traditions from the past? Explain your view.
8. What advice would you give to someone who wants to improve their English quickly?
9. Some people argue that remote work is better than office work. Do you agree? Why or why not?
10. What do you think is the most important skill for people to develop in the next ten years?

---

### Set D — Hypothetical questions (5 prompts)

1. If you could live in any country for one year, where would you go and why?
2. If you could change one thing about your education so far, what would it be?
3. If you had unlimited funding, what project or cause would you support?
4. If you could learn any skill instantly, what would you choose and how would you use it?
5. If you were asked to give a speech to 1,000 students, what would you talk about?

---

## Seven-day micro-routine

Even on non-Speaking days:

- 5 Listen and Repeat items (Sets A or B)
- 3 interview answers (any set)
- 1 minute reviewing your recording
- 1 corrected second attempt on your weakest answer

Ten focused minutes daily is more effective than one long session once a week.
