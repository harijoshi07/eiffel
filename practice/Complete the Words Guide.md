## 🎯 High-Yield Rules for Complete the Words

1. **Letter Count is a Hard Boundary**: If a box has `4L` (4 letters), your answer **must** be exactly 4 letters. If you type 3 or 5 letters, it cannot be correct.
2. **Context & Part of Speech First**: Look before and after the blank. Is it preceded by a modal (`can`, `may`)? It needs a base verb. Preceded by `the`? Likely a noun.
3. **Common Suffixes to Watch**:
   - Noun: `-tion`, `-ment`, `-ity`, `-ance`, `-ence`, `-ure`
   - Adjective: `-ive`, `-ic`, `-ical`, `-ous`, `-ed`
   - Verb: `-ate`, `-en`, `-ise / -ize`, `-ed`, `-ing`, `-es`
   - Adverb: `-ly`
4. **Function Words**: Often 1 to 3 letters: `to`, `in`, `on`, `at`, `of`, `by`, `is`, `as`, `and`, `but`, `the`, `he`, `she`, `they`, `them`.
