## Full simulation

[Launch Practice Test 2 on ETS](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/prepare/sample-test-jan-2026-2.html)

Use the interactive version because Listening and Speaking depend on audio delivery. Start at approximately the same time of day as your real appointment.

## Setup

- [ ] Quiet room and headphones
- [ ] Keyboard
- [ ] Scratch paper and pencil
- [ ] Voice recorder if needed
- [ ] Notifications disabled
- [ ] Approximately two uninterrupted hours
- [ ] No answer keys or section playbooks open

## Section targets

| Section | Published base time |
|---|---:|
| Reading | about 30 min |
| Listening | about 29 min |
| Writing | about 23 min |
| Speaking | about 8 min |

Follow the on-screen clock in the interactive version.

## Simulation rules

1. No pausing, replaying, dictionaries, or outside help.
2. Complete all four sections in order.
3. Do not review answers between sections.
4. Record Writing and Speaking responses.
5. Note technical problems separately from language errors.

## Review sequence

After a short break:

1. Check Reading against [[answer-keys/Reading Key.pdf]].
2. Check Listening against [[answer-keys/Listening Key.pdf]].
3. Read transcripts only after scoring Listening.
4. Check Build a Sentence against [[answer-keys/Writing Key.pdf]].
5. Score extended Writing with [[Writing Playbook]].
6. Score Speaking recordings with [[Speaking Playbook]].
7. Select exactly three priorities for Day 7.
