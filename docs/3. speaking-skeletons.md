# Speaking Skeletons & Delivery Blueprints

## 1. The Core Law: Creator vs. Reporter

The fundamental rule that prevents scoring penalties across the four tasks:

```
                   THE TWO ROLES OF TOEFL SPEAKING
                                  │
         ┌────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┐
         ▼                                                 ▼
   TASK 1: CREATOR                                   TASKS 2, 3, 4: REPORTER
   • 100% your own opinion                           • 0% your own opinion
   • Pronouns allowed: "I", "me", "my"               • Forbidden: "I think", "In my view"
   • Source: Your imagination/life                   • Source: Reading passage & audio clip
   • Evaluated on: Personal fluency & logic          • Evaluated on: Reporting accuracy & synthesis
```

## 2. Master Tasks Comparison

| Task | Input Modalities | Prep / Speak | Your Role | Core Content Breakdown |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Task 1** | Prompt only | 15s / 45s | **Creator** | Stance (6s) + Reason 1 (18s) + Reason 2 (18s) |
| **Task 2** | Read + Audio | 30s / 60s | **Reporter** | Reading Context (12s) + Student 2 Reasons (48s) |
| **Task 3** | Read + Audio | 30s / 60s | **Reporter** | Concept Definition (12s) + Professor Example (48s) |
| **Task 4** | Audio only | 20s / 60s | **Reporter** | Topic Frame (10s) + Sub-point 1 (24s) + Sub-point 2 (24s) |

## 3. Fill-in-the-Blank Skeletons

### Task 1: Independent Speaking (15s Prep | 45s Speak)

```
[0:00 – 0:06]  "In my opinion, I definitely believe that [Option A] is better for two main reasons."
[0:07 – 0:24]  "First of all, [Reason 1]. For example, [specific personal detail or illustration]."
[0:25 – 0:42]  "Secondly, [Reason 2]. Specifically, [second detail or concrete explanation]."
[0:43 – 0:45]  "And that is why I prefer [Option A]." (Optional buffer)
```
*Scratch Paper Rule:* During the 15s prep, write only 3 words: `Choice`, `Reason 1 Keyword`, `Reason 2 Keyword`.

---

### Task 2: Campus Situation (30s Prep | 60s Speak)

```
[0:00 – 0:12]  "According to the announcement, the university plans to [Change]. However, 
               the woman [or man] disagrees with this decision for two main reasons."
[0:13 – 0:35]  "First of all, she points out that [Reason 1]. She explains that [specific detail / story]."
[0:36 – 0:58]  "Second, she argues that [Reason 2]. Furthermore, she mentions that [second detail / example]."
[0:58 – 1:00]  "For these reasons, she opposes the new plan." (Optional buffer)
```
*Scoring Rule:* Reading is only 15% of your score; the conversation is 85%. Never spend more than 12 seconds on the reading notice.

---

### Task 3: Academic Concept (30s Prep | 60s Speak)

```
[0:00 – 0:12]  "The reading defines [Concept Name], which is when [1-sentence simple definition from text]."
[0:13 – 0:35]  "In the lecture, the professor illustrates this concept with the example of [Story / Animal / Experiment]. 
               He explains that [initial setup / action]."
[0:36 – 0:58]  "Furthermore, when [next event occurred], [outcome / result]. 
               This clearly demonstrates [Concept Name]."
[0:58 – 1:00]  "And that is how the example illustrates the concept." (Optional buffer)
```

---

### Task 4: Academic Lecture (20s Prep | 60s Speak)

```
[0:00 – 0:10]  "The professor discusses [Main Academic Topic], explaining two specific [types / adaptations / ways]."
[0:11 – 0:34]  "The first [adaptation] is [Point 1]. To illustrate this, the professor describes [details of example 1]."
[0:35 – 0:58]  "The second [adaptation] is [Point 2]. In this case, [details and outcome of example 2]."
[0:58 – 1:00]  Natural stop. (No conclusion needed)
```
