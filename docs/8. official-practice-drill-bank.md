# Official Practice Drill Bank (Post-July 2023 Authentic ETS Items)

All materials below are extracted directly from the official ETS practice sets stored locally in `/resources`.

## Drill 1: Speaking Task 1 (Independent — Explain a Choice)
- **Time Limits:** 15 seconds preparation | 45 seconds speaking
- **Authentic ETS Prompt:**
  > *"Some people enjoy taking risks and trying new things. Others are not adventurous; they are cautious and prefer to avoid danger. Which behavior do you think is better? Explain why."*
- **Execution Blueprint:** Use your [[3. speaking-skeletons#Task 1 Independent Speaking 15s Prep 45s Speak|Task 1 Skeleton]]:
  - `0–6s`: Stance ("In my opinion, I definitely believe that being cautious is better for two main reasons.")
  - `7–24s`: Reason 1 + Concrete scenario ("First of all... For example...")
  - `25–42s`: Reason 2 + Concrete scenario ("Secondly... Specifically...")
  - `43–45s`: Buffer finish.

## Drill 2: Writing Task 2 (Academic Discussion)
- **Time Limit:** 10 minutes total | Target: 120–150 words
- **Course & Professor:** Political Science — Dr. Gupta
- **Authentic ETS Prompt:**
  > **Dr. Gupta:** *"Governments make public policies to describe their responses to various problems that affect a community. Part of this process involves setting priorities about which issues deserve the most attention and resources. For example, governments need to decide whether they should spend more money on education or on environmental protections. If you were a policy maker, which issue would you argue is more important—education or environmental protections? Why?"*
  > 
  > **Kelly:** *"We all live on planet Earth, and it is the only planet we have. Therefore, we must take care of it. Clearly, protecting the environment should be the government's priority over education. I think the real question is which approach—restricting pollution, regulating population, or promoting clean energy—should be the priority."*
  > 
  > **Andrew:** *"I disagree with Kelly that the environment is more important than education. Education is actually the best way to protect the environment. Educated people can see how their decisions affect the world around them, and better science education helps develop solutions. Therefore, government should spend more on education."*
- **Execution Blueprint:** Use your [[4. writing-skeletons#3 The 3-Part Fill-in Formula|Task 2 3-Part Formula]]:
  - Part 1: Dialogue anchor (acknowledge Kelly or Andrew + state your stance).
  - Part 2: Novel contribution + concrete real-world example (not mentioned by Kelly or Andrew).
  - Part 3: Broader concluding impact statement.

## Drill 4: Writing Task 1 (Integrated Synthesis)
- **Topic:** Ecocertified Wood
- **Reading:** 3 arguments claiming American wood companies will reject international ecocertification.
- **Lecture:** Professor systematically refutes all 3 arguments.
- **Pacing:** 20 minutes to type a 4-paragraph counterpoint synthesis (220–270 words).
