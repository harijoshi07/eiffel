# Seven-Day Masterclass

## How to use this plan

Budget **3–4 focused hours per day**. If you have less time, complete the blocks marked **Core** before the optional work. Use [[Progress Tracker]] to record accuracy and recurring errors.

Every study session follows the same loop:

1. **Attempt under time pressure.**
2. **Check the answer or replay your recording.**
3. **Name the cause of each error.**
4. **Redo the item correctly without help.**
5. **State one rule that prevents the error next time.**

---

## Day 1 — Official Baseline

**Goal:** Learn the test interface and establish a clean four-section baseline.

### Core

- [ ] Read [[00 - Test Map]] — 15 min
- [ ] Read [[Practice Test 1 Guide]] — 10 min
- [ ] Complete Official ETS Practice Test 1 without interruption — about 2 hours
- [ ] Take a short break — 15 min
- [ ] Score Reading, Listening, and Build a Sentence — 30 min
- [ ] Review Writing and Speaking responses — 30 min
- [ ] Record recurring causes in [[Progress Tracker]] — 20 min

### Error labels

`spelling` · `grammar` · `vocabulary` · `missed detail` · `bad inference` · `main idea` · `time`

### Exit standard

- Every section is completed under realistic conditions.
- Errors are classified by cause rather than copied from the key.
- The two weakest task types are identified for Days 2–5.

---

## Day 2 — Listening

**Goal:** Convert speech into intent, structure, and usable notes.

### Core

- [ ] Study [[Listening Playbook]] — 35 min
- [ ] Drill 20 short response items — 20 min
- [ ] Complete one timed mixed Listening set — 30 min
- [ ] Review once with transcript, if provided — 35 min
- [ ] Complete a second set without pausing or replaying — 30 min
- [ ] Reconstruct each talk from your notes — 20 min

### Optional

- Shadow 10 minutes of clear academic speech.
- Write one-line summaries after five short audio clips.

### Exit standard

- Identify the speaker’s purpose before focusing on details.
- Notes capture structure, not complete sentences.
- Recognize corrections, contrasts, examples, and recommendations.

---

## Day 3 — Writing

**Goal:** Produce grammatical sentences and complete both extended tasks within time.

### Core

- [ ] Study [[Writing Playbook]] — 40 min
- [ ] Build 20 sentences — 25 min
- [ ] Write two timed emails — 2 × 7 min
- [ ] Review and rewrite both emails — 25 min
- [ ] Write two timed academic discussion responses — 2 × 10 min
- [ ] Edit using the playbook checklists — 25 min

### Error labels

`word order` · `verb form` · `missing prompt point` · `unclear purpose` · `weak support` · `repetition` · `time`

### Exit standard

- No inversion inside embedded questions.
- Every email addresses every bullet in the prompt.
- Every discussion response states a position and develops a reason with a concrete example.

---

## Day 4 — Speaking

**Goal:** Improve repetition accuracy, intelligibility, and interview development.

### Core

- [ ] Study [[Speaking Playbook]] — 35 min
- [ ] Record 20 Listen and Repeat attempts — 30 min
- [ ] Compare each attempt with the source — 25 min
- [ ] Answer 12 interview questions under time pressure — 35 min
- [ ] Review the recordings and repeat weak answers — 30 min

### Recording review

Score each answer from 0–2:

- **Delivery:** understandable, steady, appropriately paced
- **Language:** accurate grammar and usable vocabulary
- **Development:** direct answer plus relevant support

### Exit standard

- Preserve meaning and major content words in repetitions.
- Begin interview answers directly, without long filler.
- Develop answers with a reason, example, or consequence.

---

## Day 5 — Mixed Precision

**Goal:** Eliminate your two most expensive error patterns.

### Core

- [ ] Review [[Progress Tracker]] and select two weaknesses — 15 min
- [ ] Weakness drill 1 — 45 min
- [ ] Weakness drill 2 — 45 min
- [ ] Complete a timed mixed mini-test — 60 min
- [ ] Review and redo every miss — 35 min

### Precision rule

Do not merely do more questions. Build a targeted set:

- vocabulary confusion → contrast near-synonyms in sentences;
- missed listening details → note only names, numbers, changes, and causes;
- weak email completeness → underline each required function before writing;
- hesitant speaking → use short clauses and finish each sentence cleanly.

---

## Day 6 — Full Simulation

**Goal:** Rehearse the entire approximately two-hour experience.

### Before

- Read [[Practice Test 2 Guide]] without previewing the questions.
- Use Official ETS Practice Test 2.
- Match your planned test-day start time.
- Use headphones, a keyboard, scratch paper, and no interruptions.
- Do not pause, replay, use a dictionary, or check answers.

### Simulation

- [ ] Reading
- [ ] Listening
- [ ] Writing
- [ ] Speaking

### Review

Take a short break, then:

1. Record objective Reading and Listening results.
2. Score Writing and Speaking with the checklists in their playbooks.
3. Identify no more than three final priorities.
4. Redo only the failed patterns.

Do not take a second full test on the same day.

---

## Day 7 — Consolidate and Taper

**Goal:** Make correct performance easy and arrive rested.

### Core

- [ ] Review your error rules — 20 min
- [ ] Complete one short set from each section — 45 min total
- [ ] Write one email or discussion response — 10 min
- [ ] Record five repetitions and three interview answers — 15 min
- [ ] Read the test-day checklist below — 10 min

Stop heavy preparation at least several hours before sleep.

### Test-day checklist

- [ ] Confirm ETS identification and check-in requirements.
- [ ] Confirm test time, location, route, or home-testing setup.
- [ ] Prepare accepted identification.
- [ ] Test permitted equipment and headphones where applicable.
- [ ] Eat familiar food and hydrate normally.
- [ ] Arrive or sign in early.

## Emergency two-hour version

If a day collapses, complete:

1. 35 minutes on the day’s primary section.
2. 25 minutes reviewing errors.
3. 20 minutes on your weakest section.
4. 20 minutes of Writing or Speaking output.
5. 20 minutes reviewing rules and vocabulary.

Next: open the playbook assigned to the current day.
