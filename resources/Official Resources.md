## Resource filter — how to evaluate third-party materials

Before using any book, video, PDF, or course, check whether it includes:

- ✅ Complete the Words and Read in Daily Life
- ✅ Listen and Choose a Response and Announcements
- ✅ Build a Sentence and Write an Email
- ✅ Listen and Repeat and Take an Interview
- ✅ The 1–6 score scale

**Reject for format simulation if it centres on:**
- ✗ Integrated Writing (reading + lecture → essay)
- ✗ Four legacy Speaking tasks
- ✗ Two long 700-word Reading passages as the complete section
- ✗ The 0–120 / 0–30 per-section scoring scale

Older material can still build vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and academic comprehension. Label it as **language drills**, not format practice.

## One-vault resource rule

Do not collect dozens of sources. Use:

1. This vault for strategy and drills.
2. The two ETS sample tests for format simulation.
3. TOEFL TestReady for official feedback.
4. Your error log for targeted review.

That is sufficient material for a complete 8-week preparation.
