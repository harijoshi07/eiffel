# Official Resources

Use these ETS pages as the source of truth. Links were verified for the format used on and after January 21, 2026.

   Official task types, item counts, and approximate base times.

   Explains the 1–6 scale, overall-score calculation, CEFR alignment, and transitional comparable score.

   Official practice portal with a free practice test and performance feedback.

## Resource filter

Before using a book, video, PDF, or course, check whether it includes:

- Complete the Words and Read in Daily Life;
- Listen and Choose a Response and announcements;
- Build a Sentence and Write an Email;
- Listen and Repeat and Take an Interview;
- the 1–6 score scale.

If it instead centers on Integrated Writing, four legacy Speaking tasks, two long Reading passages, or the 0–120 scale alone, it teaches the pre-January-2026 format.

Older resources may still help with vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, or academic comprehension. Label them as **language drills**, not current-format simulations.

## One-week resource rule

Do not collect dozens of sources. Use:

1. this masterclass for strategy;
2. the two current ETS sample tests for format;
3. TOEFL TestReady for official feedback;
4. your error log for targeted review.

That is enough material for one disciplined week.
