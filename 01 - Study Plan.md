# 8-Week Study Plan — Target: 5.5

**Format:** TOEFL iBT 2026 · **Goal:** 5.5 / 6 overall (≥ 5 each section, ≥ 6 on your strongest)

## How to use
- 60–90 focused minutes a day. Core items first, optional only if time allows.
- After every session, add the result to the section log: `logs/reading.md`, `logs/listening.md`, `logs/writing.md`, or `logs/speaking.md`.
- Sources: [[resources/pdfs/full-length-1.pdf]] and [[resources/pdfs/full-length-2.pdf]] (full tests), `resources/pdfs/teacher-resources-1.pdf` to `-5.md` (extra practice), `resources/pdfs/guide-*.md` (writing and listening guides). Audio is in `resources/audio/`.

## Daily micro-routine (all 8 weeks, 35 min)
| Min | Task |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Read one short English text. Write a one-sentence summary. |
| 10 | Play one audio clip once without replaying. Write the main idea and two details. |
| 10 | Record one 60-second speaking answer. Play it back and score it. |
| 5 | Add 5 words to your vocabulary list, each with a sentence. |

## Phase 1 — Orientation (Week 1)
| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Read [[00 - Test Map]]. Set your target band. |
| 2 | Complete **Full-Length Test 1** under timed conditions (no pausing). |
| 3 | Score Reading and Listening. Self-score Writing and Speaking with the rubric in `resources/pdfs/guide-writing-rubrics.pdf`. Log the baseline in each section log. |
| 4 | Pick your two weakest sections. |
| 5 | Confirm your test date. |
| 6 | 30 min of your weakest section. |
| 7 | Review. Write your Week 1 priorities at the top of this file. |

**Exit:** baseline in all four sections; the two task types costing the most points identified.

## Phase 2 — Section focus (Weeks 2–5)

### Week 2 — Reading (`logs/reading.md`)
| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Complete the Words: candidate check (see logs/reading.md). 20 items, timed. |
| 2 | Read in Daily Life: find purpose first. 10 items, timed. |
| 3 | Read an Academic Passage: paragraph map, then questions. One passage. |
| 4 | Mixed timed set: 5 Complete the Words + 5 Daily Life + 1 Academic Passage. |
| 5 | Review every miss. Write the cause and a prevention rule for each. |
| 6 | Second mixed timed set. Cut the error count from Day 4. |
| 7 | Update the reading log with three reading rules. |

**Exit:** 80%+ accuracy on each task type.

### Week 3 — Listening (`logs/listening.md`, `resources/audio/lessons/listening/`)
| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Listen and Choose a Response: three checks. 20 short clips. |
| 2 | Conversations: take minimal notes. Reconstruct two conversations from the notes. |
| 3 | Announcements: capture the action. Three announcements. |
| 4 | Academic talks: structure first, then details. Two talks, each reconstructed in 20 seconds. |
| 5 | Mixed set: 10 responses + 1 conversation + 1 announcement + 1 talk. |
| 6 | Review every miss in the listening log. |
| 7 | Update the listening log with three listening rules. |

**Exit:** state a talk's main purpose within 5 seconds of its end.

### Week 4 — Speaking (`logs/speaking.md`, `resources/audio/samples/speaking/`)
| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Listen and Repeat: chunking and recovery. 20 items, compare with the source. |
| 2 | 20 more repeats. Find your three most common error types. |
| 3 | Interview: PREP structure. 10 questions, 60 seconds each, all recorded. |
| 4 | 12 more interview questions. Score each one. |
| 5 | Replay your five worst recordings. Redo each once. |
| 6 | Full 8-minute speaking mock (11 items). Score and log. |
| 7 | Update the speaking log with three speaking rules. |

**Exit:** every answer starts within 2 seconds; repeats keep the meaning and the key content words.

### Week 5 — Writing (`logs/writing.md`, `resources/pdfs/guide-writing-rubrics.pdf`)
| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Build a sentence: word order. 30 items. |
| 2 | 30 more sentence items, focusing on embedded questions. |
| 3 | Write an email: two timed emails. |
| 4 | Two more emails. Self-review against the rubric. |
| 5 | Academic discussion: two timed responses. |
| 6 | Two more responses. Score each 0–5 with the rubric. Rewrite anything below 4. |
| 7 | Update the writing log with three writing rules. |

**Exit:** emails address every point and state the purpose in the first sentence; discussion responses give a position, a reason, and an example.

## Phase 3 — Integration (Weeks 6–7)

### Week 6 — Full simulation and weak-area focus
| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Full-Length Test 2**, no pausing. |
| 2 | Score every section. Log it. |
| 3 | Drill the section farthest from 5.5 for 90 minutes. |
| 4 | Second 90-minute session on the same section. |
| 5 | Mixed drill: 30 min per section. |
| 6 | Review the logs. Choose three final priority rules. |
| 7 | Rest. Confirm test logistics. |

### Week 7 — Timed precision (daily 2-hour block)
| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Reading | 30 min | 1 mixed set; aim for zero errors |
| Listening | 30 min | 1 mixed set; aim for zero errors |
| Writing | 30 min | 1 email + 1 academic discussion |
| Speaking | 20 min | 5 repeats + 5 interview answers |
| Review | 10 min | Log one lesson per section |

**Precision rule:** if you miss the same item type twice, drill that type alone before moving on.

## Phase 4 — Taper (Week 8)
| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Review all 12 rules (three per section). |
| 2 | Light set from each section, 20 min total. |
| 3 | One email and one discussion, no timer. |
| 4 | Five repeats and three interview answers. Replay and check delivery. |
| 5 | Read the test-day checklist. |
| 6 | Rest. No new material. |
| 7 | **Test day.** |

### Test-day checklist
- [ ] Photo ID matches the registration name.
- [ ] Confirm time, location, or home-test setup.
- [ ] Headphones and microphone tested (home test).
- [ ] Scratch paper and pencil ready.
- [ ] Eat, hydrate, and sleep on your normal schedule.
- [ ] Sign in 30 minutes early.

## Emergency 60-minute version
1. 20 min: today's section, timed.
2. 15 min: yesterday's errors.
3. 15 min: weakest section.
4. 10 min: write or speak one answer.
