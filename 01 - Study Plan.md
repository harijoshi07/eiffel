# 8-Week Study Plan — Target: 5.5

**Format:** TOEFL iBT 2026 · **Goal:** 5.5 / 6 overall (≥ 5 each section, ≥ 6 on your strongest)

---

## How to use this plan

- Budget **60–90 focused minutes per day minimum**.
- Mark Core items first. Do Optional only if time allows.
- After every session, write one row in [[daily/Day Log]].
- After every error, write one row in [[workbook/Progress Tracker]] error log.

---

## Phase 1 — Orientation (Week 1)

**Goal:** Understand the exact test and know your starting level.

| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Read [[00 - Test Map]] completely. Set your target band and institution requirement in [[workbook/Progress Tracker]]. |
| 2 | Complete **ETS Sample Test 1** under timed conditions (no pausing, no replaying). [[practice/test-1/README]] |
| 3 | Score Reading and Listening. Self-score Writing and Speaking with playbook checklists. Record baseline in [[workbook/Progress Tracker]]. |
| 4 | Identify your **two weakest sections**. Read their playbooks. |
| 5 | Read [[resources/Official Resources]]. Bookmark the ETS links. Confirm your test date. |
| 6 | Do 30 minutes of your weakest section using [[drills/]] materials. |
| 7 | Rest or light review. Update the Progress Tracker with your Week 1 priorities. |

**Exit standard:** You know your baseline band in all four sections. You have identified the two task types costing you the most points.

---

## Phase 2 — Section Focus (Weeks 2–5)

Each week has a primary section. You still touch all four sections daily via the micro-routine.

### Daily Micro-Routine (every day, all 8 weeks)

| Minutes | Task |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Read one short English text (news, Wikipedia, email). Write a one-sentence summary. |
| 10 | Listen to one audio clip once without replaying. Write: main idea + two details. |
| 10 | Record one 60-second speaking answer. Play it back and score it. |
| 5 | Add 5 new words to your vocabulary list with a sentence for each. |

---

### Week 2 — Reading

**Playbook:** [[sections/Reading Playbook]] · **Drills:** [[drills/Reading Drills]]

| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Study Complete the Words — four-pass method. Do 20 drill items timed. |
| 2 | Study Read in Daily Life — read-by-function method. Do 10 mini-texts timed. |
| 3 | Study Read an Academic Passage — paragraph map + question playbook. Do one passage. |
| 4 | Mixed timed set: 5 Complete-the-Words + 5 Daily Life + 1 Academic Passage. |
| 5 | Review all errors. Write cause + prevention rule for each miss. |
| 6 | Second mixed timed set. Aim to cut error count from Day 4. |
| 7 | Update Progress Tracker. Write your three Reading rules. |

**Exit standard:** 80%+ accuracy on each task type. Paragraph map completed within 2 minutes of reading.

---

### Week 3 — Listening

**Playbook:** [[sections/Listening Playbook]] · **Drills:** [[drills/Listening Drills]]

| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Study Listen and Choose a Response — three checks. Drill 20 short clips. |
| 2 | Study Conversation — minimal notes template. Reconstruct two conversations from notes. |
| 3 | Study Announcement — action-capture template. Drill three announcements. |
| 4 | Study Academic Talk — structure-before-detail. Drill two talks and reconstruct each in 20 seconds. |
| 5 | Mixed set: 10 Choose-a-Response + 1 Conversation + 1 Announcement + 1 Academic Talk. |
| 6 | Review. Diagnose every miss with the error table in the Listening Playbook. |
| 7 | Update Progress Tracker. Write your three Listening rules. |

**Exit standard:** Can state the speaker's main purpose within 5 seconds of a talk ending. Notes are readable in 10 seconds.

---

### Week 4 — Speaking

**Playbook:** [[sections/Speaking Playbook]] · **Drills:** [[drills/Speaking Drills]]

| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Study Listen and Repeat — chunking and recovery. Record 20 items. Compare to source. |
| 2 | Drill 20 more Listen-and-Repeat items. Identify your three most common error types. |
| 3 | Study Take an Interview — PREP structure. Answer 10 questions, 60 sec each, record all. |
| 4 | Answer 12 more interview questions. Score each with the self-review rubric. |
| 5 | Replay your worst five recordings. Redo each once correctly. |
| 6 | Full 8-minute Speaking mock (11 items). Score and log. |
| 7 | Update Progress Tracker. Write your three Speaking rules. |

**Exit standard:** Every interview answer begins within 2 seconds. Repetitions preserve meaning and key content words. Self-score ≥ 5 / 6 in Intelligibility and Relevance.

---

### Week 5 — Writing

**Playbook:** [[sections/Writing Playbook]] · **Drills:** [[drills/Writing Drills]]

| Day | Core work |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Study Build a Sentence — construction order + trap checklist. Do 30 sentence items. |
| 2 | Drill 30 more Build-a-Sentence items. Focus on embedded-question word order. |
| 3 | Study Write an Email — seven-minute workflow + checklist. Write two timed emails. |
| 4 | Write two more timed emails. Peer-review or self-review against checklist. |
| 5 | Study Academic Discussion — reliable structure + ten-minute workflow. Write two timed responses. |
| 6 | Write two more timed responses. Self-score each with the 0–10 rubric. Rewrite anything below 8. |
| 7 | Update Progress Tracker. Write your three Writing rules. |

**Exit standard:** Emails: all prompt points addressed, purpose clear in first sentence. Academic Discussion: position + reason + example in every response.

---

## Phase 3 — Integration (Weeks 6–7)

### Week 6 — Full Simulation + Weak-Area Focus

| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Full mock test** — ETS Sample Test 2, no pausing. [[practice/test-2/README]] |
| 2 | Score all sections. Record in Progress Tracker. |
| 3 | Identify the section still farthest from 5.5. Spend 90 min drilling it. |
| 4 | Second 90-min drill session on the same weak section. |
| 5 | Mixed drill: 30 min per section. |
| 6 | Review error log. Select three final priority rules. |
| 7 | Rest. Confirm test logistics if test is within two weeks. |

---

### Week 7 — Timed Precision

Every day this week runs the same 2-hour block:

| Block | Time | Content |
|---|---:|---|
| Reading | 30 min | 1 mixed set — aim for zero errors |
| Listening | 30 min | 1 mixed set — aim for zero errors |
| Writing | 30 min | 1 Email + 1 Academic Discussion |
| Speaking | 20 min | 5 Repeats + 5 interview answers |
| Review | 10 min | Log one lesson per section in Day Log |

**Precision rule:** Do not simply add volume. If you miss the same item type twice, isolate it and drill it in isolation before moving on.

---

## Phase 4 — Taper (Week 8)

**Goal:** Arrive rested with rules consolidated.

| Day | Task |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Review all three rules from each section (12 rules total). |
| 2 | Light short set from each section — 20 min total. |
| 3 | Write one email and one academic discussion (no timer pressure). |
| 4 | Record five repetitions + three interview answers. Replay and confirm delivery. |
| 5 | Read the test-day checklist below. |
| 6 | Rest. No new material. |
| 7 | **Test day.** |

### Test-day checklist

- [ ] Photo ID matches registration name exactly.
- [ ] Confirm test time, location, or home-testing setup.
- [ ] Test headphones and microphone (home testing).
- [ ] Scratch paper and pencil ready.
- [ ] Eat familiar food, hydrate, sleep by your normal time.
- [ ] Arrive or sign in 30 minutes early.

---

## Emergency 60-minute version

If a day collapses, do at minimum:

1. 20 min — today's assigned section, timed.
2. 15 min — review errors from yesterday.
3. 15 min — your weakest section.
4. 10 min — write or speak one output.
