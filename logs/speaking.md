## Tips
- Listen and repeat: capture the meaning first, then the content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives, numbers), then grammar words (articles, endings), then stress and rhythm.
- Interview: use PREP. P = Point (answer first). R = Reason. E = Example. P = Point again. Keep it to one clear idea.
- Compare each recording with the sample answer in `resources/audio/samples/speaking/`.
