2. **Purpose and inference (Q13).** 3 of 4 Daily Life misses. You picked a true detail or a word copied from the passage.

## Complete the Words: per blank
1. **Count letters.** The word must have exactly N letters.
2. **Write the word family:** base, -s, -ed, -ing, -tion/-ity/-ment, plural.
3. **Check the slot:**
   - after *the / a / this* → noun
   - in a list "X, Y, and Z" → plural noun
   - starting a sentence with an action → -ing form
   - after *to / can / may* → base verb
4. **Check meaning:** structures are built things, processes are changes that happen, procedures are steps someone follows.
5. **Check the next word** for collocation.
6. **If two words pass, check the next word.** Otherwise choose the one that passes all checks.

## Daily Life: per question
1. **Mark the type:** Purpose (why / what is this for), Inference (what can be inferred / implied), Detail (what / how many).
2. **Answer in one line before reading the options.**
   - Purpose: "The email is to ___."
   - Count: "Total − the named person."
3. **Pick the option that matches your line.** Reject options that only repeat a passage word.
4. **For Q13:** the sentence that states the reason decides the purpose. Details support it.
