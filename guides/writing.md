## Tips
- Write an email (rubric 5 = fully successful): give enough detail to support the purpose, use polite conventions for requests and refusals, and keep errors rare (no typos like there/their).
- Academic discussion (rubric 5 = fully successful): make a relevant contribution, explain it with an example or detail, and vary sentence structure.
- Reread for the rubric points above before you submit.
