# Listening: how to improve

Strategies and tips for Listening. Your attempts and mistakes are recorded in `logs/listening.md`. When a pattern shows up in the log, add a rule here.

## Tips
- Three checks for "Listen and Choose a Response": (1) speech act: asking, inviting, apologizing, suggesting, confirming, or complaining? (2) grammar: does the answer fit the question form? (3) situation: would a real person say it next?
- Traps: same topic, no logical answer; "where" answered with a time; ignoring implied meaning or polite refusal.
- Conversations and talks: attitude/opinion questions use phrases like "What is the speaker's attitude toward X?" Listen for the expression that introduces the opinion.
- Academic talk: follow the structure first, then the details. Note only key words and numbers.
