## Slot → form table (V4 = -ing)

| Slot in the sentence | Form needed |
|---|---|
| After *to*, or after a modal (*can, will, may, should*) | V1 (base) |
| After *have/has/had* | V3 (past participle) |
| After *is/are/was/were* + action | V4 (-ing) |
| After *is/are/was/were* + action done to something | V3 (passive) |
| After a preposition (*by, without, in, about*) | V4 (-ing) or noun |
| Sentence-start subject doing an action | V4 (gerund) |
| After *the/a/this/that*, or an adjective | Noun |
| Verb with *he/she/it* as subject | V-s |
| Object of a verb with no article | Plural noun |
